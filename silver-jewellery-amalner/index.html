Why silver is priced differently from gold

Silver is cheaper per gram, so the making charge is a larger share of what you pay. That is normal, but it is worth knowing before you compare two shops on the sticker price alone. Ask two questions: what is the silver rate today, and what is the making per gram? We answer both at the counter.

Ask us which purity a piece is before you buy — the mark is on the item, and the difference between a jewellery alloy and fine silver matters for anything that will touch skin daily or be used in pooja.

Looking after silver

Silver tarnishes — that is chemistry, not a fault. Store it dry, away from perfume and rubber, and wipe it with a soft cloth rather than a harsh polish. Heavier tarnish comes off with a polish at the shop; bring the piece in and we will clean it.
