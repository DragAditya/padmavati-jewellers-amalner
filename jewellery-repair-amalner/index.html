Jewellery repair in Amalner

Broken chains, tired polish, a ring that no longer fits — done in our own hands.

The short answer

Padmavati Jewellers repairs jewellery in Saraf Bazar, Amalner. Work is done in-house: chain soldering, ring resizing, re-polishing, stone tightening and small jobs of that kind. The piece is weighed and its condition noted before we take it, and the charge is told to you before work starts.

What we repair

  • Chains and clasps. Broken links, stretched chains, worn or missing clasps, and safety hooks added.
  • Ring resizing. Up or down a size, on plain bands and on stone-set rings where the setting allows it.
  • Bangles and kada. Dents taken out, joints re-soldered, size adjusted where the design permits.
  • Earring posts and backs. Bent posts straightened, backs replaced, posts thickened.
  • Re-polishing. A tired piece brought back to a fresh finish, in gold or silver.
  • Stone tightening. Loose stones re-set or tightened before one falls out.

If the job is something not on this list, bring the piece in and ask. Repair work is varied, and the honest answer is often available only once we have it in hand.

How a repair works

  1. Bring the piece in. We look at it with you and say whether the repair is straightforward or whether it needs something we cannot do properly.
  2. Weigh and note the condition. Weight and any existing damage are written down before the piece leaves your hands, so there is no argument later.
  3. Agree the charge and the date. You hear the making charge and the collection date before work starts. If it changes mid-job, we call you first.
  4. Collect and check. The piece is weighed back and checked with you, and any change in weight is explained.

What we will not do

We will not take a piece we cannot repair properly, and we will not promise a same-day finish on work that needs longer. If a stone is too damaged to reset, or a chain too thin to solder reliably, we say so before you leave it with us. That is worth more to you than a promise we cannot keep.

Selling instead of repairing

Sometimes a piece is not worth fixing. If that is what we think, we will say it — and you can weigh the old gold against a new piece instead. Old gold buying and exchange.

Repair questions

How much does jewellery repair cost in Amalner?

It depends on the work — a chain solder is priced differently from a full re-polish or a resize with stones. Bring the piece in and we will quote the charge before any work starts.

How long does a repair take?

Simple work such as a chain solder or a clasp replacement is often done the same day or the next. Resizing and re-polishing take longer, and you get a collection date when you leave the piece.

Can a ring be resized if it has stones?

Often yes, but not always. It depends on the setting and how far the size has to change. We check the piece first and tell you honestly whether resizing risks the stones.

Do you polish silver and gold?

Yes. Bring the piece in and we will polish it. Light tarnish usually comes off the same day.
