Before you come in

For a rate, call or WhatsApp. Gold moves through the day, so a number read on a website this morning may already be out of date.

For a repair, bring the piece. Photos help, but a quote needs the weight and the condition in front of us.

For a bridal set, bring the wedding date, the outfit colours and any family piece you want matched. If the wedding is in season, come a few weeks ahead.

For old gold, bring any bill you still have. It speeds up the purity check, though it is not compulsory.

Looking for diamonds, please note we do not sell them — we keep gold, silver, bridal and pooja silver.

Directions from around Amalner

Saraf Bazar runs through the centre of Amalner, close to the bus stand and the main market. From the bus stand, head towards the old market and ask for Saraf Bazar — anyone will point you to it. We are at the Aadva end, on the ground floor, with parking on the road outside.

Coming from Chopda, Dharangaon, Parola, Erandol or further out, allow extra time on market days, when the street is busiest.
