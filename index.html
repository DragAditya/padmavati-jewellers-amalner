Do you buy old gold in Amalner?

Yes. Old gold is tested and weighed in front of you, then priced at the day's rate for its purity. You can take the value as cash or against a new piece.

Do you sell diamonds?

No. We sell gold, silver and bridal jewellery. If you want a diamond piece, we would rather tell you plainly than have you travel here for something we do not keep.

How do I know the purity of what I am buying?

Ask what karat it is — 22K (916), 24K (999) or 18K (750) — and look for the stamp on the piece. The karat and net weight are written on the bill. Where a piece carries a BIS hallmark, its six-digit HUID can be checked free on the BIS Care app before you pay.
