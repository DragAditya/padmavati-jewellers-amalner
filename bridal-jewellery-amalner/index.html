Wedding sets

Bridal jewellery in Amalner

The full set, built around the wedding you are actually having — not the one on the poster.

The short answer

Padmavati Jewellers sells and makes bridal jewellery in Saraf Bazar, Amalner, including 22K gold wedding sets and Maharashtrian traditional pieces such as nath, thushi, gajra and patlya. Sets can be tried before purchase and made to order from your design. Polki and kundan work is available when we can source it, otherwise it is ordered.

What a bridal order looks like here

A wedding set is not one purchase, it is a list: what the bride wears, what her mother gives, what the in-laws bring, and what stays in the locker for the next generation. We plan it that way, so the budget goes where it will be seen and the rest is bought for weight rather than show.

The Maharashtrian set

Nath, thushi, gajra, vajratik, patlya and bangles in 22K. These are regional pieces with regional proportions, and getting the scale right matters more than adding another item to the list.

Polki and kundan

We keep polki and kundan work when it is available, and we order it when it is not. Tell us early if the design depends on it, because sourcing takes time and we would rather say so at the start than two weeks before the wedding.

Plain gold sets

If the family prefers traditional plain gold, the set is built from the pieces above with no stone work at all — easier to maintain, and easier to value at the time of a future exchange.

How an order runs

  1. Look and try. Come in with the family. Trying a set on a bride tells you more than any photograph.
  2. Fix the list and the budget. We write down the pieces, the approximate weight and the total, so nothing is added quietly later.
  3. Agree weight, purity and making. Each piece gets a confirmed weight, karat and making charge before work begins.
  4. Confirm the date. We give you a delivery date in writing. Wedding season is busier, so the earlier you come the safer that date is.
  5. Final fitting and billing. The set is weighed and checked with you, and the bill shows gross, stone and net weight.

Booking early is the whole trick

Between November and February the workshop is full. A set ordered well ahead can be made exactly as drawn; the same set ordered in the last ten days usually means compromising on weight or design. If the date is fixed, start the conversation early — a booking costs nothing and reserves the slot.

What to bring

Bring the wedding date, the bride's outfit colours, any family piece you want matched or reused, and a rough budget. If you have a photo from a magazine or a relative's wedding, bring that too — it is faster than describing a design in words.

Bridal questions

Can we try the bridal set before buying?

Yes. Come in with the family and try the set on. If you order a new design, we arrange a trial before the final fitting.

Do you make Maharashtrian traditional sets?

Yes — nath, thushi, gajra, vajratik, patlya and bangles in 22K gold, in the proportions traditional in this part of Jalgaon district.

Do you have polki and kundan sets?

When we can source them, yes — and if we cannot, we order them. Because sourcing takes time, tell us early if the design depends on it rather than leaving it to the last weeks.

How long does a made-to-order set take?

It depends on the piece and the season. We give you a delivery date in writing when the design, weight and making charge are confirmed, and we tell you honestly if the date is tight.

Can we exchange old family gold towards the set?

Yes. Old gold is tested and weighed in front of you at the day's rate, and the value goes against the new set. That is how many families here fund a wedding purchase.
