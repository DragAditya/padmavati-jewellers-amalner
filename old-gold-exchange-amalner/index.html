Sell or exchange old gold in Amalner

Tested and weighed in front of you, at the day's rate — cash, or against something new.

The short answer

Padmavati Jewellers buys old gold in Saraf Bazar, Amalner, and also takes it in exchange against new jewellery. Old gold is tested for purity and weighed in front of you, then priced at the day's rate. You choose whether to take cash or set the value against a new piece — exchange usually works out better.

What we take

Old jewellery, broken pieces, single earrings, coins and bars. Age and condition do not matter much — what matters is the purity and the weight, and both are established in front of you rather than across a counter in a back room.

How the value is worked out

  1. Purity. The piece is tested, because an old bangle may be 22K, 20K, or a mixed alloy. Stamps help, but testing is what settles it.
  2. Weight. Weighed on the scale in front of you, with stones and beads removed from the figure.
  3. Rate. The day's rate for that purity — the same rate the shop sells at, not a separate buying number invented on the spot.
  4. Cash or exchange. Take the value as cash, or set it against a new piece.

Why exchange usually beats cash

When you put old gold against a new piece, the value of your gold goes straight into the new purchase instead of sitting in the shop's cash. In practice that means you are usually offered better terms than a straight cash sale. Ask for both figures and compare — we will give you both, on paper, before you decide.

Bring the bill if you have one

It is not compulsory, but an old bill answers the purity question faster than testing alone. If the piece came from here, we may still have the record.

Selling gold and the rate

Buyback uses the same market rate as a sale. What reduces the figure is the refining needed to bring old jewellery back to pure gold, and that depends on the piece — solder, stones and mixed alloys all count. We explain the deduction before you agree to it, not after. How the Amalner rate works.

What to expect at the counter

Bring the gold, ask for the purity test and the weight to be shown to you, and ask for the rate per gram being applied. If a number is not explained to your satisfaction, do not sell. That is true here as much as anywhere.
