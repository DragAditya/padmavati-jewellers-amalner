Why the rate changes every day

Gold is imported and priced in US dollars per ounce, then converted to rupees per 10 grams. Two things move the Indian rate: the international gold price, and the rupee against the dollar. A wedding season spike or a festival like Dhanteras adds local demand on top.

Amalner is a jewellery town, so the local rate sits close to the Mumbai rate. The difference between shops is rarely the gold rate itself — it is the making charge, the wastage and how the purity is declared. Those are the numbers worth comparing.

From the market rate to your bill

  1. Market rate per gram for the purity you are buying — 24K, 22K or 18K.
  2. Multiplied by net weight — the weight after stone and bead weight is removed.
  3. Plus making charge — a percentage, a per-gram figure or a wastage percentage, whichever applies to the piece. You hear it before you buy.
  4. GST, only on a GST invoice — 3% on the total of gold value and making charge, printed as a separate line. Without the invoice, the quoted price stands.

Three ways people get overcharged

Paying gold rate on stone weight. If the stone weight is not removed, you pay gold price for glass or resin. Ask for the net weight.

Hidden wastage. Wastage should be a figure you were told about, not a new line that appears at billing.

No purity mark. Without a hallmark and HUID there is nothing to verify later. At 22K, that check is worth more than a small discount.

Gold rate questions

What is the 22K gold rate in Amalner today?

The 22K rate changes daily with the Mumbai bullion market. Call or WhatsApp the shop for the current figure — we confirm the rate that applies on the day you buy.

Why is the Amalner rate different from the Mumbai rate?

Amalner tracks Mumbai closely, with a small adjustment for local demand and transport. The bigger difference between shops is usually the making charge, not the gold rate.

Will I be charged GST on gold?

The price we quote is what you pay. If you need a GST invoice, 3% GST is added on the gold value plus making charge and shown as a separate line. Ask for it when you buy.

Should I buy gold on Dhanteras?

Dhanteras is a traditional buying day, and demand pushes availability and making charges more than the gold rate. If you want a specific piece or weight, ask a few weeks earlier.
