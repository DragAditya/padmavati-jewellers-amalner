About Padmavati Jewellers

A jewellery shop on the old saraf street, run the way that street has always run.

The short answer

Padmavati Jewellers is a family-run gold and silver jewellery shop at Aadva, Saraf Bazar, Amalner, in Jalgaon district, Maharashtra. It sells gold and silver jewellery, bridal sets, coins and pooja silver, repairs jewellery in-house, and buys or exchanges old gold. It holds a 4.8 rating from 32 Google reviews.

The shop

Saraf Bazar is the old jewellery street in the middle of Amalner, and it has been the place this town buys gold for generations. Padmavati Jewellers sits at the Aadva end of it. The shop is small enough that the person who sells you a chain is the person who weighs it, and the person who fixes it if it breaks.

Rajendra Wagh is the owner. Sanket Sonar manages the shop day to day.

How we work

Three habits decide how a purchase goes here.

Everything is weighed in front of you. Gross weight, stone weight, net weight. If a number is going to appear on the bill, it appears on the scale first.

The making charge is told before the work. On a new piece, on a repair, on a made-to-order set. If it has to change mid-job, you get a call before it does.

You get a bill if you want one. Ask for a GST invoice and 3% is added and printed with the weight and karat. That invoice is what a buyback, an exchange or a gold loan will ask for years later, so it is worth asking for even when the amount is small.

What we sell

  • Gold jewellery in 24K, 22K and 18K — chains, mangalsutra, bangles, rings, temple pieces.
  • Silver jewellery — payal, anklets, kada and bangles.
  • Silver utensils and pooja items — arti plates, diyas, gangajal pots, coins.
  • Gold coins and bars — 24K, sealed, lowest making charge.
  • Bridal jewellery — wedding sets and Maharashtrian traditional pieces, made to order.
  • Repair — soldering, resizing, polishing, stone tightening, in-house.
  • Old gold — bought at the day's rate, or exchanged.

What we do not sell

We do not sell diamonds, imitation jewellery or watches. If you are looking for those, you are better served by a shop that specialises in them — and you will not have made the trip to Saraf Bazar for nothing.

Who we serve

Most customers come from Amalner itself, and from the towns and villages around it — Chopda, Dharangaon, Parola, Erandol, Jalgaon, Shirpur, Dhule and the villages between them. Many are second- and third-generation customers whose parents bought here first, which is the only credential in this trade that really counts.

Trust, in writing

  • Karat (22K, 24K, 18K) and net weight printed on the bill, after stone and bead weight is removed.
  • Net weight calculated after stone and bead weight is removed.
  • Making charge explained before purchase, before repair, before a custom order.
  • GST invoice on request — 3% added, with weight and karat printed.
  • A public Google rating of 4.8 from 32 reviews, which we do not filter or delete. Read them here.
